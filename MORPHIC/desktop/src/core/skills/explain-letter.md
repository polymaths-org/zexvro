---
name: explain-letter
description: Explain a letter, bill, notice or document in simple words.
triggers: letter, bill, notice, statement, document, invoice, explain this, what does this mean, pension, tax, insurance, electricity, policy
---
1. Start with one sentence: what this document is and who sent it.
2. Then the three things that matter most, in plain words: what it says, whether anything must be paid or done, and by when.
3. If there is something to do, show it as a checklist with show_plan, and offer to set a reminder before any deadline.
4. Point out anything that looks unusual or worrying (very high amounts, threats, requests for codes).
5. Never guess numbers that aren't in the text; say if something is unclear.
