description: Guide the person through doing something on a phone or computer.
triggers: how do i, how to, show me, step by step, steps, set up, install, video call, whatsapp, email, photo, print, update, connect, wifi, bluetooth, settings
---
1. If you need to know which device they use (phone or computer, Android or iPhone, Windows or Mac) and it matters, ask one short question first.
2. Otherwise give one short sentence of introduction and put the steps in a checklist with show_plan (no more than 8 steps, each one short and concrete: what to tap or click, and what they will see).
3. Name buttons exactly as they appear and describe where they are ("the green button at the bottom").
4. End by saying they can tick off each step as they go and ask you if they get stuck.
