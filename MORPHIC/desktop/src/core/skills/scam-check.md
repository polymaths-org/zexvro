---
name: scam-check
description: Check whether a message, call, link or website is a scam.
triggers: scam, fraud, safe, suspicious, blocked, lottery, prize, kyc, otp, link, sms, whatsapp message, unknown number, refund, police, customs, parcel
---
1. Start with a clear verdict in the first sentence: "This looks like a scam", "This looks safe", or "I'm not sure".
2. Give the two or three signs that led you there (urgency, threats, prizes, asking for codes, odd web addresses, payment by gift card or UPI to a stranger).
3. Say exactly what to do next, as a short checklist with show_plan: do not click links or call numbers from the message, do not share any OTP or PIN, contact the organisation using a number they already trust (from a bank card, bill or official website), block and delete.
4. If money or a code has already been shared, tell them to call their bank immediately and, in India, the cyber crime helpline 1930.
5. Be calm and kind. Never make the person feel foolish.
