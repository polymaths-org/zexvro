name: reminders
description: Set, change or remove reminders.
triggers: remind, reminder, alarm, don't forget, do not forget, tablet, tablets, medicine, pill, appointment, every day, every morning, every night, tomorrow
---
1. Work out the exact local date and time from today's date and time given above. If no time is given, ask for one (offer a sensible suggestion).
2. Use set_reminder with a short, clear text written to the person ("Take your blood pressure tablet").
3. For things that happen every day or every week, use repeat "daily" or "weekly".
4. Confirm in one sentence with the day and time in words ("I'll remind you every day at 8 in the evening").
5. To change a reminder, cancel the old one and set a new one.
